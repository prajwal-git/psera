# Assets

`assets/collection.jpg` is original AI-generated concept jewellery imagery created with the built-in image-generation tool for this app. It is not a photograph of actual PSERA stock. Generation prompt: luxury jewellery studio still life with a sculptural gold necklace, gold hoops, pearl strand, ivory travertine and linen, soft mauve background, sunlight, landscape framing and negative space for copy; no logos, text or watermarks.

Product/category imagery is drawn in Flutter by `JewelleryArt` in `lib/ui/common.dart`. These are original demo illustrations; no product imagery or branding was copied from Pyrite Fashion's screenshots. Replace them with authorised real product images before launch.

Manrope and Playfair Display fonts are bundled for offline consistency. Their SIL Open Font License texts are in `assets/fonts/`. Sources are the official Google Fonts repository:
- https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope
- https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/playfairdisplay

App icons use a simple original PSERA “p” monogram. The supplied screenshots were used only to understand shopping navigation and layout.
